## Purpose

A property under construction earns nothing while it is being built, but the
construction loan still accrues interest. The interest reserve is the pool of
loan proceeds set aside at closing to pay that interest until the property
produces enough cash to pay it. Without one, the borrower funds interest out of
pocket.

## Inputs

Draw Schedule (1.1.13). The loan interest rate and the length of the reserve
period.

## Output

One figure sized at closing, and a periodic draw against that balance on the
model's period grid.

## Method

Project the outstanding loan balance in each period from the draw schedule.
Apply the interest rate to that balance to get the interest owed in the period.
Sum interest across the reserve period. That sum is the reserve.

## Rules

1. `Interest Reserve = sum of projected interest over the reserve period`
2. Projected interest in a period is the period's opening loan balance times
   the periodic interest rate.
3. The reserve is funded from loan proceeds. It appears on both sides of
   Sources and Uses (1.1.14).
4. An interest draw increases the outstanding loan balance in the period it is
   drawn, so subsequent periods accrue interest on it.
5. The reserve period ends at the earlier of reserve exhaustion or the first
   period in which Cash Flow from Operations (1.2.12) covers Debt Service
   (1.2.13).
6. Interest accruing after the reserve period is Debt Service (1.2.13), not
   this line.
