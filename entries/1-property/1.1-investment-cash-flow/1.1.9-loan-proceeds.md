## Purpose

Loan proceeds are the amount the lender advances to fund the deal. They are
the largest source of funds in a levered deal, the starting balance of the
loan (1.2.17), and the amount the loan ratios are measured against.

## Inputs

Acquisition Cost (1.1.1). The advance rate, as a percentage of price. For a
development, the Draw Schedule (1.1.13).

## Output

One figure, in the model's currency: the total advanced over the life of the
loan.

## Method

Multiply the acquisition cost by the advance rate. For a development, add up
the draws.

## Rules

1. `Loan Proceeds = Acquisition Cost (1.1.1) x advance rate`
2. Gross amount. Lender fees are a use of funds and are not deducted here
   (1.1.8, Rule 3).
3. An acquisition loan is funded in full at the acquisition date. A
   development loan is advanced over time, and Loan Proceeds is the sum of the
   draws in the Draw Schedule (1.1.13).
4. Sized on the price, not on Total Investment Cost (1.1.8), so the loan does
   not depend on the fees it pays.
5. Zero where the deal is unlevered.

## Worked example

An acquisition funded in full at closing, with a 65% advance rate.

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| Acquisition Cost (1.1.1) | 7,500,000 |
| Advance rate | 65% |
| **Loan Proceeds** | **4,875,000** |
