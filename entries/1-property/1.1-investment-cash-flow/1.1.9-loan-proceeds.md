## Purpose

Loan proceeds are the amount the lender advances to fund the deal. They are
the largest source of funds in a levered deal, and the amount the loan ratios
and the amortization schedule (1.2.17) start from.

## Inputs

The sizing basis, usually Acquisition Cost (1.1.1). The advance rate, as a
percentage of that basis.

## Output

One figure, in the model's currency: the amount advanced at closing.

## Method

Multiply the sizing basis by the advance rate.

## Rules

1. `Loan Proceeds = sizing basis x advance rate`. For a loan sized on price,
   the basis is Acquisition Cost (1.1.1).
2. Gross amount. Lender fees are a use of funds and are not deducted here
   (1.1.8, Rule 3).
3. An acquisition loan is funded in full at the acquisition date. A loan
   advanced in stages follows the Draw Schedule (1.1.13).
4. A loan sized on Total Investment Cost (1.1.8) depends on the lender fees
   inside that cost, and those fees depend on the loan. Solve the two
   together.
5. Zero where the deal is unlevered.

## Worked example

An acquisition funded in full at closing, with a 65% advance rate on price.

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| Acquisition Cost (1.1.1) | 7,500,000 |
| Advance rate | 65% |
| **Loan Proceeds** | **4,875,000** |
