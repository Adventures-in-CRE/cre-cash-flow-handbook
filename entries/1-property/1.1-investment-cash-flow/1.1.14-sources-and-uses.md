## Purpose

Sources and uses is the schedule that states where the money for a deal comes
from and what it is spent on. It is the first thing a capital partner reads and
the first thing that reveals an error, because the two sides must agree to the
cent.

## Inputs

Total Investment Cost (1.1.8). Loan Proceeds (1.1.9). Equity Required at
Acquisition (1.1.11). Mezzanine Debt (1.1.15). Preferred Equity (1.1.16).

## Output

Two columns that sum to the same figure, stated once for the whole deal and
optionally per period.

## Method

List every use of funds, which is the full project or acquisition budget. List
every source, which is each debt tranche, each equity tranche, and any reserve
funded from proceeds. Size the equity as the residual so that the two sides
balance.

## Rules

1. `Total Sources = Total Uses`, exactly, in every period the schedule is
   stated.
2. Uses is Total Investment Cost (1.1.8).
3. Sources is the sum of Loan Proceeds (1.1.9), Mezzanine Debt (1.1.15),
   Preferred Equity (1.1.16), and Equity Required at Acquisition (1.1.11).
4. The interest reserve (1.1.12) appears as a use and is funded within the loan
   source. It is not netted out of either side.
5. Where the schedule is stated per period, both sides balance in every period,
   not only in total.
6. Sources are positive. Uses are positive. The schedule does not use a sign
   convention to force the balance.
