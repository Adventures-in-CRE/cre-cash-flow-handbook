## Purpose

Soft costs are the development costs that are not labor or materials. Design
fees, permits, legal, insurance, marketing, financing fees, and the developer
fee. They are easy to underestimate, because they arrive in many small pieces
across the whole project rather than under one contract. Also called indirect
costs.

## Inputs

Each soft cost line with its own basis. Some are fixed amounts, some are a
percentage of hard costs, some accrue per period held.

## Output

One figure for the project, and a periodic stream across the development
period.

## Method

Model each line on its own basis. Fixed fees are stated as amounts. Fees quoted
against construction are a percentage of Hard Costs (1.1.3). Time-based costs
accrue in each period the project is held.

## Rules

1. `Soft Costs = sum of the indirect development costs in the budget`
2. Includes architecture and engineering, permits and entitlement fees, legal,
   insurance, marketing and lease-up, property tax during construction,
   financing fees, and the developer fee.
3. Excludes physical construction, which is Hard Costs (1.1.3).
4. Excludes the interest reserve (1.1.12), which is stated separately because it
   is sized off the draw schedule rather than off the budget.
5. Lines quoted as a percentage of hard costs are computed before contingency
   (1.1.5) unless the entry states otherwise.
6. Feeds Total Investment Cost (1.1.8).
