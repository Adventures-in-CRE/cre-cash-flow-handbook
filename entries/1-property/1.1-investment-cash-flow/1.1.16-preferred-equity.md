## Purpose

Preferred equity is capital that sits between debt and common equity. It earns
a fixed return and is paid ahead of common equity but behind every loan. It
fills the gap between what a lender will lend and what the sponsor will fund,
without adding a lien on the property.

## Inputs

The preferred commitment, its rate, its payment priority, and whether it
participates in upside beyond its rate.

## Output

One contribution figure at closing, and a periodic distribution stream on the
model's period grid.

## Method

Add the preferred contribution to sources at closing. Accrue its return each
period on the unreturned balance. Pay that return from cash available after
debt service, before any common equity distribution. Repay the balance at sale
or refinance, before common equity.

## Rules

1. Preferred equity proceeds are a source of funds in Sources and Uses
   (1.1.14).
2. Preferred equity is equity, not debt. It is excluded from Debt Service
   (1.2.13), Loan-to-Value (1.5.9), and Debt Service Coverage Ratio (1.5.8).
3. The preferred return accrues on the unreturned balance each period. Unpaid
   amounts accrue and are paid before common equity in a later period.
4. Preferred equity has no maturity date unless the deal terms state one.
5. Preferred Equity is a position in the capital stack. Preferred Return
   (3.10.3) is a distribution threshold inside a partnership. They are
   different things and are not modeled in the same place.
