## Purpose

Hard costs are what it costs to physically build the building: materials,
labor, sitework, and finishes. They are the largest line in a development
budget and the most directly countable, because every item maps to something
you can point at on site. Also called direct costs.

## Inputs

The construction contract or the trade-by-trade budget. The building program
and its area.

## Output

One figure for the project, and a periodic stream across the construction
period.

## Method

Take the contract sum, or the budget trade by trade. Spread it across the
construction period on the schedule the work is expected to be performed.

## Rules

1. `Hard Costs = sum of the direct construction costs in the budget`
2. Includes materials, labor, sitework, site utilities, and interior finishes.
3. Excludes design fees, permits, financing costs, and the developer fee, which
   are Soft Costs (1.1.4).
4. Excludes the contingency held against them, which is Contingency (1.1.5).
5. Spread across the construction period on the schedule the work occurs, which
   is what drives the Draw Schedule (1.1.13).
6. Retainage withheld from a trade payment is recorded when it is released, not
   when the work was performed.
7. Feeds Total Investment Cost (1.1.8).
