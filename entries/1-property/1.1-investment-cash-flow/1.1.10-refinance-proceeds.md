## Purpose

A refinance replaces the existing loan with a new one. Where the new loan
exceeds the balance being retired, the difference is returned to the owner. It
lets an owner take capital out of a property without selling it, which resets
leverage and changes every levered return in the model. Also called a cash-out
refinance.

## Inputs

The new loan amount. Loan Amortization (1.2.17), for the balance being retired.
Refinancing costs.

## Output

One figure at the refinance date, in the model's currency.

## Method

Size the new loan against value or cash flow at the refinance date. Subtract the
outstanding balance on the existing loan and the costs of the refinance. What
remains is distributed to equity.

## Rules

1. `Refinance Proceeds = new loan amount - outstanding balance retired -
   refinancing costs`
2. Recorded in the period the refinance closes.
3. The outstanding balance comes from Loan Amortization (1.2.17) at that date,
   and includes any prepayment penalty or yield maintenance owed on retirement.
4. The new loan begins its own amortization schedule from the refinance date.
5. Appears in Net Levered Cash Flow (1.4.2). It never appears in Net Unlevered
   Cash Flow (1.4.1).
6. A refinance raising less than the balance retired requires an equity
   contribution, and the figure is negative.
