## Purpose

Closing and due diligence costs are the one-time costs a buyer pays to
complete the purchase, on top of the price: legal, title and escrow,
inspections and third-party reports, and transfer taxes. They are part of what
the investment costs, but they buy nothing the property earns income on.

## Inputs

Acquisition Cost (1.1.1). A closing cost rate, or a fixed amount.

## Output

One figure, at the acquisition date, in the model's currency.

## Method

Multiply the acquisition cost by the closing cost rate. Where the amount is
known, enter it directly.

## Rules

1. `Closing & Due Diligence Costs = Acquisition Cost (1.1.1) x closing cost
   rate`, or the fixed amount where one is entered.
2. The rate is a single percentage of the price. A model may instead itemize
   the costs and sum them.
3. Excludes lender fees and other financing costs, capital expenditures
   (1.1.6), and the interest reserve (1.1.12). Those are separate costs.
4. Paid at the acquisition date and included in Total Investment Cost
   (1.1.8).
5. Not recovered at sale. Costs of selling are separate (1.3.3).

## Worked example

Continuing the example in 1.1.1, with closing costs at 1.00% of the price.

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| Acquisition Cost (1.1.1) | 7,500,000 |
| Closing cost rate | 1.00% |
| **Closing & Due Diligence Costs** | **75,000** |
