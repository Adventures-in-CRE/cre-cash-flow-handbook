## Purpose

Mezzanine debt is a loan that sits between the senior mortgage and the equity.
It is secured by a pledge of the ownership interest in the entity that owns the
property rather than by the property itself, which lets the mezzanine lender
foreclose on the entity quickly if the borrower defaults. It costs more than
senior debt because it is repaid after it.

## Inputs

The mezzanine commitment, its interest rate, its amortization terms, and its
position in the capital stack.

## Output

One proceeds figure at closing, and a periodic payment stream on the model's
period grid.

## Method

Add the mezzanine proceeds to sources at closing. Compute its periodic payment
on its own terms, separate from the senior loan. Pay it after senior debt
service and before any equity distribution.

## Rules

1. Mezzanine proceeds are a source of funds in Sources and Uses (1.1.14).
2. Mezzanine payments are part of Debt Service (1.2.13) and are paid after
   senior debt service in the same period.
3. Where interest accrues rather than pays currently, the accrued amount is
   added to the mezzanine balance and repaid at Loan Payoff (1.3.5).
4. Mezzanine debt is included in Loan-to-Cost (1.5.10) and excluded from
   Loan-to-Value (1.5.9) unless the entry for that metric states otherwise.
5. Debt Service Coverage Ratio (1.5.8) is stated separately for senior debt
   alone and for senior plus mezzanine. The two are not interchangeable.
