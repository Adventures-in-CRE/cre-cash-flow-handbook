## Purpose

A construction loan is not advanced in full at closing. The lender releases it
in pieces as the project is built. The draw schedule states how much of the
loan is drawn in each period, and it is what turns a project budget into a
financing cash flow with dates on it.

## Inputs

Hard Costs (1.1.3). Soft Costs (1.1.4). Contingency (1.1.5).

## Output

A periodic stream of loan advances on the model's period grid, plus a running
outstanding loan balance.

## Method

Spread the budget across the construction period in the pattern the work is
expected to be performed. In each period, apply the equity contribution first
where the loan requires equity ahead of debt, then draw the remainder from the
loan. Carry the cumulative drawn amount forward as the outstanding balance.

## Rules

1. `Period draw = period costs incurred - period equity contribution`
2. Cumulative draws never exceed the committed loan amount.
3. Draws are positive to the borrower and increase the outstanding loan
   balance in the period drawn.
4. Where the loan requires equity to fund first, equity funds in full before
   the first draw. Where it requires pari passu funding, each period splits by
   the loan-to-cost ratio (1.5.10).
5. Interest paid from the interest reserve (1.1.12) is a draw and increases the
   balance.
6. Costs withheld as retainage are drawn in the period they are released, not
   the period the work was performed.
7. Excludes permanent loan proceeds, which are Loan Proceeds (1.1.9).
