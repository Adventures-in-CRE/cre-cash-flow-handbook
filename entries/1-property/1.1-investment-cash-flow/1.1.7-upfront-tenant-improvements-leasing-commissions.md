## Purpose

These are the leasing costs an owner commits to at acquisition: space already
vacant that has to be leased, and leases already signed whose allowances have
not yet been paid. They are known at closing, which is what separates them from
the rollover costs that arrive later in the hold.

## Inputs

Vacant area at acquisition and its lease-up assumption. Outstanding allowances
and commissions on signed leases. The allowance rate and the commission rate.

## Output

One figure, and a periodic stream over the lease-up period.

## Method

For vacant space, apply the allowance rate to the area and the commission rate
to the rent over the assumed new lease term. Add allowances and commissions
already committed on signed leases and not yet paid.

## Rules

1. `Cost = area x allowance rate + total rent over the new term x commission
   rate`, for space leased under the acquisition plan.
2. Includes obligations on leases signed before closing that the buyer assumes.
3. Recorded in the period the lease commences, or in the period of payment
   where the obligation is already committed.
4. Costs driven by leases rolling during the hold are Tenant Improvements and
   Leasing Commissions (1.2.11), not this line.
5. Feeds Total Investment Cost (1.1.8).
