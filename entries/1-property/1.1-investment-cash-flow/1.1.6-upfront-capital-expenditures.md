## Purpose

Upfront capital expenditures are the capital work an owner commits to at or
shortly after acquisition. Deferred maintenance, a unit renovation program, a
roof. They are part of what the deal costs rather than part of what it earns,
which is why they sit in the investment section and not in operations.

## Inputs

The condition assessment or renovation scope. Cost per item or per unit. The
schedule over which the work is performed.

## Output

One figure for the program, and a periodic stream over the work period.

## Method

Price the scope, by item or per unit. Spread it over the periods in which the
work is performed.

## Rules

1. `Upfront Capital Expenditures = sum of the scoped capital items`
2. A capital expenditure is an expenditure with a useful life greater than one
   year. Costs consumed within the year are operating expenses (1.2.8).
3. Recorded in the periods the work is performed, not entirely at closing,
   unless the entry states a single upfront outlay.
4. Recurring capital work across the hold is Capital Expenditure Reserves
   (1.2.10). Work in the acquisition or renovation scope is this line.
5. Tenant-driven work is Upfront Tenant Improvements and Leasing Commissions
   (1.1.7).
6. Feeds Total Investment Cost (1.1.8).
