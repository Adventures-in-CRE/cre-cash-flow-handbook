## Purpose

Equity required at acquisition is the cash the owners put in at closing. It is
what remains of the total cost after the lenders and any other capital have
paid their share. It is the balancing figure of sources and uses (1.1.14) and
the base for cash-on-cash return (1.5.6).

## Inputs

Total Investment Cost (1.1.8). Loan Proceeds (1.1.9). Mezzanine Debt (1.1.15).
Preferred Equity (1.1.16).

## Output

One figure, at the acquisition date, in the model's currency.

## Method

Subtract every other source of funds from the total investment cost.

## Rules

1. `Equity Required at Acquisition = Total Investment Cost (1.1.8) - Loan
   Proceeds (1.1.9) - Mezzanine Debt (1.1.15) - Preferred Equity (1.1.16)`
2. A residual. It is set to make sources equal uses (1.1.14), not entered.
3. Common equity only. Preferred Equity (1.1.16) is a separate source and is
   subtracted above, not counted here.
4. Lines that do not apply are zero. Without debt, it equals Total Investment
   Cost.
5. Covers closing only. Equity contributed later to fund operating shortfalls
   is not included, so it can be less than the total equity a deal ends up
   needing.

## Worked example

Continuing the example in 1.1.8 and 1.1.9, with no mezzanine debt or
preferred equity.

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| Total Investment Cost (1.1.8) | 7,723,750 |
| Loan Proceeds (1.1.9) | (4,875,000) |
| **Equity Required at Acquisition** | **2,848,750** |
