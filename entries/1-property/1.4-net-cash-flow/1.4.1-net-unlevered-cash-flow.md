## Purpose

Net unlevered cash flow is the whole deal in one row, before any debt. It is
what the property costs to buy or build, what it produces while it is held, and
what it returns when it is sold. Every unlevered return metric is solved off
this row, so its composition has to be exact.

## Inputs

Total Investment Cost (1.1.8). Cash Flow from Operations (1.2.12). Net Reversion
Proceeds (1.3.4).

## Output

One figure per period, in the model's currency, on the model's period grid.

## Method

Record the investment as an outflow in the periods it is funded. Add operating
cash flow in each period. Add net sale proceeds in the disposition period.

## Rules

1. `Net Unlevered Cash Flow = Cash Flow from Operations (1.2.12) - Total
   Investment Cost (1.1.8) + Net Reversion Proceeds (1.3.4)`, each taken in the
   period it falls.
2. Investment outflows are negative. Operating and reversion inflows are
   positive.
3. Excludes loan proceeds (1.1.9), debt service (1.2.13), refinance proceeds
   (1.1.10), and loan payoff (1.3.5). All financing belongs to Net Levered Cash
   Flow (1.4.2).
4. Excludes sponsor fees (3.15) and promote (3.10.5).
5. Feeds Gross Unlevered IRR (1.5.1) and Gross Unlevered Equity Multiple
   (1.5.2).
6. Where the model runs unlevered only, this is the final cash flow row.
