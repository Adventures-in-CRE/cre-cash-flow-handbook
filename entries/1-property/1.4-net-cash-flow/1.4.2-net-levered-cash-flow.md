## Purpose

Net levered cash flow is the whole deal in one row, after debt. It is what the
equity actually funds, what it receives while the property is held, and what is
left once the lender is repaid at sale. Every levered return metric is solved
off this row.

## Inputs

Total Investment Cost (1.1.8). Loan Proceeds (1.1.9). Refinance Proceeds
(1.1.10). Cash Flow after Financing (1.2.14). Reversion Before-Tax Cash Flow
(1.3.6).

## Output

One figure per period, in the model's currency, on the model's period grid.

## Method

Record the investment net of loan proceeds as an outflow in the periods it is
funded. Add operating cash flow after debt service in each period. Add refinance
proceeds when they occur, and net sale proceeds after loan payoff at
disposition.

## Rules

1. `Net Levered Cash Flow = Cash Flow after Financing (1.2.14) - (Total
   Investment Cost (1.1.8) - Loan Proceeds (1.1.9)) + Refinance Proceeds
   (1.1.10) + Reversion Before-Tax Cash Flow (1.3.6)`, each in the period it
   falls.
2. Loan proceeds reduce the equity outflow in the period drawn. They are not
   recorded as a separate inflow.
3. Reversion is stated after Loan Payoff (1.3.5), which is what Reversion
   Before-Tax Cash Flow (1.3.6) already carries.
4. Excludes sponsor fees (3.15) and promote (3.10.5).
5. Feeds Gross Levered IRR (1.5.4) and Gross Levered Equity Multiple (1.5.5).
6. Where the model runs unlevered only, this row is not produced.
