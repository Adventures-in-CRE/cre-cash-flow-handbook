## Purpose

Effective gross income is the revenue the property is actually expected to
collect. It is potential gross income after every revenue deduction and plus
every non-rent source. Operating expenses are measured against it and management
fees are usually computed off it, so it is the revenue figure the rest of the
model uses. Also called effective gross revenue.

## Inputs

Potential Gross Income (1.2.1). Loss to Lease (1.2.2). Vacancy Loss (1.2.3).
Concessions (1.2.4). Credit and Collection Loss (1.2.5). Other Income (1.2.6).
Expense Reimbursements (1.2.15).

## Output

One figure per period, in the model's currency, on the model's period grid.

## Method

Start with potential gross income. Subtract each revenue deduction. Add other
income and expense reimbursements.

## Rules

1. `Effective Gross Income = Potential Gross Income (1.2.1) - Loss to Lease
   (1.2.2) - Vacancy Loss (1.2.3) - Concessions (1.2.4) - Credit and Collection
   Loss (1.2.5) + Other Income (1.2.6) + Expense Reimbursements (1.2.15)`
2. Computed once per period, on the model's period grid.
3. Deductions are stated as positive amounts and subtracted. They are not
   carried as negative revenue.
4. Every deduction appears on its own line. A model that nets them into a single
   adjustment does not conform.
5. Excludes operating expenses (1.2.8), which are applied below this line to
   reach Net Operating Income (1.2.9).
