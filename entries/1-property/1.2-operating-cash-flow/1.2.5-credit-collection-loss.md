## Purpose

Credit and collection loss is rent that was billed to an occupied space and
never collected. It covers tenants who fall behind, tenants who default, and
tenants who leave owing money. It is separate from vacancy because the space was
occupied and the rent was earned.

## Inputs

Potential Gross Income (1.2.1). The credit loss assumption, or the property's
own collection history.

## Output

One figure per period, in the model's currency, on the model's period grid.

## Method

Apply the credit loss rate to the rent billed on occupied space in the period.
Where a specific tenant is expected to default, model that tenant's rent
directly instead of applying a blanket rate to it.

## Rules

1. `Credit and Collection Loss = rent billed on occupied space x credit loss
   rate`
2. Deducted from Potential Gross Income (1.2.1) as a reduction of revenue.
3. Applied to occupied space only. Vacant space bills nothing and is covered by
   Vacancy Loss (1.2.3).
4. Applied after Loss to Lease (1.2.2), so the base is contract rent rather
   than market rent.
5. Recoveries of previously written off rent are recorded in the period
   received and reduce this line. They are not Other Income (1.2.6).
6. Where the model states a combined general vacancy and credit loss figure,
   the two are still reported on separate lines and the combined rate is split
   between them.
