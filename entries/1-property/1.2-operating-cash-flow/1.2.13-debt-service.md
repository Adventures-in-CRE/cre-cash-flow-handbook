## Purpose

Debt service is what the property pays its lenders each period. It is the first
claim on cash after operations and the line that separates an unlevered return
from a levered one. Every coverage test a lender runs is a comparison against
this number.

## Inputs

Loan Amortization (1.2.17), which supplies the interest and principal split.
Mezzanine Debt (1.1.15), where present.

## Output

One figure per period, in the model's currency, on the model's period grid.

## Method

Take interest and scheduled principal from the amortization schedule for the
period. Add any mezzanine payment. The total is debt service for the period.

## Rules

1. `Debt Service = interest + scheduled principal, summed across all loans in
   the period`
2. Senior debt service is paid before mezzanine debt service (1.1.15) in the
   same period.
3. On a fixed rate loan the rate is constant for the term. On a floating rate
   loan the rate is the period's index plus the spread, and it is recomputed
   each period.
4. Where an interest rate cap is in place, the floating rate used is the lesser
   of the index plus spread and the strike rate plus spread.
5. Interest funded from the interest reserve (1.1.12) during construction is not
   debt service. It is a draw against that reserve.
6. Excludes the balloon repayment at maturity, which is Loan Payoff (1.3.5), and
   prepayment penalties, which are recorded there.
7. On an unlevered analysis this line is zero.
