## Purpose

In most commercial leases the tenant pays back some share of the landlord's
operating costs. Expense reimbursements are that income. On a net leased
property the line can be a quarter of total revenue, and leaving it out
understates the cost of vacancy.

## Inputs

Operating Expenses (1.2.8), split into recoverable and non-recoverable. The
recovery structure in each lease. Occupied area by tenant.

## Output

One figure per period, in the model's currency, on the model's period grid.

## Method

Allocate the recoverable expenses to tenants by their pro rata share of
occupied area. Apply the recovery structure in each lease to that share. Sum
across tenants.

## Rules

1. `Reimbursement = tenant pro rata share x (recoverable expenses - the
   tenant's stop or base amount)`, floored at zero.
2. Pro rata share is the tenant's occupied area divided by total rentable
   area, unless the lease names a different denominator.
3. Under a base year stop, the base amount is the recoverable expenses in the
   tenant's stated base year, held flat thereafter.
4. Under an expense stop, the base amount is the fixed figure named in the
   lease.
5. Under a triple net lease the base amount is zero and the tenant reimburses
   its full share.
6. Under a full service gross lease with no stop, reimbursement is zero.
7. Vacant area recovers nothing. The landlord absorbs that share.
8. Reimbursements are revenue and feed Effective Gross Income (1.2.7). They are
   never netted against Operating Expenses (1.2.8).
