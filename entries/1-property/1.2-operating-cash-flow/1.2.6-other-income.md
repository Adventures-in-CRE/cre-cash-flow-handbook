## Purpose

Other income is property revenue that is not rent for the leased space itself
and not an expense recovery. Parking, storage, fees, and amenity charges all sit
here. It is small as a share of revenue in most property types and large enough
in some that omitting it materially undervalues the asset.

## Inputs

The revenue-producing items at the property, each with its own rate and volume
assumption.

## Output

One figure per period, in the model's currency, on the model's period grid.

## Method

Model each source on its own driver. Parking and storage are priced per space
per period against an assumed utilization. Fees are priced per unit or per
turnover event. Sum the sources.

## Rules

1. `Other Income = sum of each other income source in the period`
2. Sources are listed separately where each is material, and grouped only where
   individually immaterial.
3. Sources that scale with occupancy are driven by occupied units or area, not
   by total units or area.
4. Excludes expense recoveries, which are Expense Reimbursements (1.2.15), and
   rent for the leased space, which is Potential Gross Income (1.2.1).
5. Excludes proceeds from asset sales, financing, and insurance settlements.
   Those are not operating revenue.
6. Utility billbacks under a ratio utility billing system are a recovery of
   operating cost and belong in Expense Reimbursements (1.2.15), not here.
