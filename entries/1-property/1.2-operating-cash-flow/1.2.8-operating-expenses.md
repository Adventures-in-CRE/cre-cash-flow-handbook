## Purpose

Operating expenses are the recurring costs of running the property. They are
what stands between effective gross income and net operating income, and the
boundary around them is what makes one property's NOI comparable to another's.

## Inputs

Each expense account, with its own driver. The expense growth assumption. The
recoverable and non-recoverable split for each account.

## Output

One figure per period, in the model's currency, on the model's period grid.

## Method

Model each account on the driver that actually moves it. Fixed accounts are
stated as an amount and grown. Variable accounts are stated as a rate against
the driver they follow. Sum the accounts.

## Rules

1. `Operating Expenses = sum of each expense account in the period`
2. Fixed accounts do not change with occupancy. Insurance and real estate taxes
   are fixed unless the entry states otherwise.
3. Variable accounts change with occupancy or revenue. A management fee stated
   as a percentage of effective gross income (1.2.7) is variable.
4. Real estate taxes are assessed value times the millage rate, where one mill
   is one thousandth of the assessed value.
5. Ground rent (1.2.16) is an operating expense.
6. Each account is flagged recoverable or non-recoverable. The recoverable
   portion drives Expense Reimbursements (1.2.15), which is revenue and is never
   netted against this line.
7. Excludes capital expenditure reserves (1.2.10), tenant improvements and
   leasing commissions (1.2.11), debt service (1.2.13), income tax, and
   depreciation.
