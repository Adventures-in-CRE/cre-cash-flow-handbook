## Purpose

Gross sale price is what a buyer pays for the property at exit, before the
seller's costs of sale. It is the exit income (1.3.1) divided by the rate of
return the market requires on that income (1.5.12). It is the first step of
the reversion and the figure from which net proceeds are built.

## Inputs

Terminal Net Operating Income (1.3.1). Exit Cap Rate (1.5.12).

## Output

One figure per exit date, in the model's currency.

## Method

Divide terminal net operating income by the exit cap rate.

## Rules

1. `Gross Sale Price = Terminal Net Operating Income (1.3.1) / Exit Cap Rate
   (1.5.12)`
2. Computed once per candidate exit date. A model that prices a sale only at
   the end of the hold computes it once.
3. The exit cap rate for a given exit date is set in 1.5.12. This entry only
   divides by it.
4. Before selling costs (1.3.3) and loan payoff (1.3.5). Some models label
   this figure Net Sales Price while deducting nothing; the label does not
   change what it is.
5. Independent of debt. The same price results with or without a loan.
6. A zero or missing exit cap rate has no valid price; the model must return
   an error or blank, not zero.

## Worked example

A 10-year hold on an annual grid, continuing the example in 1.3.1, with a 6.5%
exit cap rate.

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| Terminal Net Operating Income (1.3.1) | 638,600 |
| Exit Cap Rate (1.5.12) | 6.50% |
| **Gross Sale Price** | **9,824,615** |
