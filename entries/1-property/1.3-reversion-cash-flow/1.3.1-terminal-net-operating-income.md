## Purpose

Terminal net operating income is the income a buyer capitalizes to price the
property at sale. A buyer pays for income they will receive, not income the
seller just earned, so this line takes net operating income for the twelve
months after the exit date. It is the numerator of the sale price (1.3.2).

## Inputs

Net Operating Income (1.2.9) for the periods covering the twelve months after
the exit date.

## Output

One annual figure, in the model's currency.

## Method

Sum net operating income over the twelve months after the exit date.

## Rules

1. `Terminal NOI = sum of Net Operating Income (1.2.9) over the 12 months
   after the exit date`
2. The exit date is the end of the hold. A sale at the end of year 10 uses
   year 11 net operating income.
3. On an annual grid this is one period. On a quarterly grid it is the next 4
   periods, and on a monthly grid the next 12.
4. The projection must run twelve months past the hold.
5. Uses the same definition as hold-period net operating income. Capital
   Expenditure Reserves (1.2.10), Tenant Improvements & Leasing Commissions
   (1.2.11), and Debt Service (1.2.13) are not deducted.
6. A model that prices a sale at any period recomputes this for each
   candidate exit date.
7. Varies on period type (Rule 3), not on property type or leverage.

## Worked example

A 10-year hold on an annual grid, continuing the example set in 1.2.9, where
NOI is 620,000 in the exit year. Year 11 assumes 3% growth for illustration.

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| Net Operating Income, year 10 (exit year) | 620,000 |
| Net Operating Income, year 11 | 638,600 |
| **Terminal Net Operating Income** | **638,600** |
