## Purpose

Breakeven occupancy is the occupancy at which a property covers its operating
expenses and its debt service and nothing more. Below it the owner funds the
shortfall. Lenders test it because it states how much of the rent roll can be
lost before the loan stops being paid from the property.

## Inputs

Potential Gross Income (1.2.1). Operating Expenses (1.2.8). Debt Service
(1.2.13).

## Output

A rate, stated as a percentage, for a single period.

## Method

Add operating expenses to debt service. Divide by potential gross income at
full occupancy. The result is the share of potential income the property must
collect to break even.

## Rules

1. `Breakeven Occupancy = (Operating Expenses (1.2.8) + Debt Service (1.2.13))
   / Potential Gross Income (1.2.1)`
2. All three inputs are taken from the same period.
3. Potential Gross Income is stated at full occupancy, before vacancy loss
   (1.2.3), concessions (1.2.4), and credit loss (1.2.5).
4. The result equals the occupancy at which Debt Service Coverage Ratio (1.5.8)
   equals 1.00.
5. Where the property has expense reimbursements (1.2.15), those are included in
   the denominator, because they are lost with the tenant that paid them.
6. On an unlevered analysis, debt service is zero and the result is the
   operating breakeven.
