## Purpose

Residual land value is what a developer can pay for a site and still earn the
profit the deal requires. It is derived by working backwards from the value of
the finished building, subtracting everything it costs to build and the profit
the developer needs. Whatever is left is what the land is worth to that
developer.

## Inputs

The value of the completed development. Hard Costs (1.1.3). Soft Costs (1.1.4).
The developer's required profit.

## Output

One figure, in the model's currency, for the site as a whole.

## Method

Value the completed and stabilized development. Subtract every development cost
except land. Subtract the required profit. The remainder is the residual land
value.

## Rules

1. `Residual Land Value = completed value - development costs excluding land -
   required profit`
2. Development costs excluding land are Hard Costs (1.1.3), Soft Costs (1.1.4),
   Contingency (1.1.5), and financing costs including the interest reserve
   (1.1.12).
3. Required profit is stated either as an amount or as a percentage of cost,
   and the entry states which.
4. The result is a ceiling on Acquisition Cost (1.1.1) for the site, not a
   market value.
5. Where the required profit is expressed as a target return rather than an
   amount, the residual is solved so that the return is met exactly.
6. A negative result means the site cannot support the assumed development.
