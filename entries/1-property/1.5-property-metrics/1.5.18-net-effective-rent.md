## Purpose

Net effective rent is the rent a landlord actually keeps after paying for the
deal that produced it. Face rent alone is not comparable between leases,
because one tenant took six months free and a large improvement allowance and
another took neither. Net effective rent puts the two on the same footing.

## Inputs

Contract rent over the lease term. Concessions (1.2.4). Tenant Improvements and
Leasing Commissions (1.2.11).

## Output

A rate, stated per unit of area per year, for one lease.

## Method

Total the contract rent payable over the lease term. Subtract free rent, tenant
improvement allowance, and leasing commissions. Divide the result by the lease
term and by the leased area.

## Rules

1. `Net Effective Rent = (total contract rent - free rent - tenant improvement
   allowance - leasing commissions) / lease term / leased area`
2. Computed over the full lease term, not a single period.
3. Where the calculation is stated on a discounted basis, every term is
   discounted to lease commencement at the stated rate, and the entry says so.
   An undiscounted figure is not compared against a discounted one.
4. Operating expense reimbursements (1.2.15) are excluded unless the lease is
   gross and the comparison is against other gross leases.
5. Landlord costs are subtracted in full in the period incurred, not amortized,
   unless the discounted basis is used.
