## Purpose

Weighted average lease term states how much contracted rent is left at a
property, expressed as a length of time. It is the single number that tells a
buyer or a lender how soon the income has to be re-leased. It is also called
WALT, WAULT, and WALE.

## Inputs

Each tenant's current contract rent and remaining lease term as of the
measurement date.

## Output

A duration, stated in years, at a point in time.

## Method

For each tenant, multiply current rent by remaining lease term. Sum those
products across all tenants. Divide by the sum of current rent across the same
tenants.

## Rules

1. `WALT = sum(tenant rent x tenant remaining term) / sum(tenant rent)`
2. Remaining term is measured from the measurement date to lease expiry, in
   years.
3. Rent is contract rent in the current period, not market rent and not
   straight-lined rent.
4. Vacant space is excluded from both the numerator and the denominator.
5. Renewal options are excluded unless the option has been exercised.
6. Where area is used as the weight instead of rent, the entry states so. The
   two produce different answers and are not interchangeable.
7. Stated at a point in time, not per period.
