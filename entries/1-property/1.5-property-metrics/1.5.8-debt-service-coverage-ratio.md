## Purpose

The debt service coverage ratio is net operating income divided by debt service.
It states how much cushion the property has over what it owes the lender.
Lenders set a floor on it, and the loan is sized to whatever amount keeps the
ratio above that floor.

## Inputs

Net Operating Income (1.2.9). Debt Service (1.2.13).

## Output

A multiple, stated to two decimal places, per period.

## Method

Divide net operating income for the period by debt service for the same period.

## Rules

1. `DSCR = Net Operating Income (1.2.9) / Debt Service (1.2.13)`
2. Both terms come from the same period on the same grid.
3. Stated as a multiple. A ratio of 1.00 means the property covers debt service
   exactly and has nothing left.
4. The numerator is net operating income, which is before capital expenditure
   reserves (1.2.10) and before tenant improvements and leasing commissions
   (1.2.11). A lender underwriting after those states so.
5. Where mezzanine debt (1.1.15) is present, the ratio is stated for senior debt
   alone and for total debt.
6. The occupancy at which this ratio equals 1.00 is Breakeven Occupancy
   (1.5.14).
7. Also called DSC.
