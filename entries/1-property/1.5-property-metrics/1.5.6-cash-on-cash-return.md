## Purpose

Cash-on-cash return is the cash an equity investor actually receives in a period
divided by the equity they have put in. It answers the question an owner asks
first, which is what the deal pays them this year on the check they wrote.

## Inputs

Cash Flow after Financing (1.2.14). Equity Required at Acquisition (1.1.11).

## Output

A rate, stated as a percentage per year, for a single period.

## Method

Take cash flow after financing for the period, which is operating cash flow less
debt service. Divide by total equity contributed through that period. Annualize
where the period is shorter than a year.

## Rules

1. `Cash-on-Cash Return = Cash Flow after Financing (1.2.14) / total equity
   contributed to date`
2. Total equity contributed to date is Equity Required at Acquisition (1.1.11)
   plus equity funded in later periods, measured cumulatively at the period
   being reported.
3. The numerator is before tax and after debt service (1.2.13).
4. Excludes reversion proceeds. It measures operations only.
5. Stated per period, and annualized where the grid is shorter than a year.
6. The unlevered equivalent is Free and Clear Return (1.5.3).
