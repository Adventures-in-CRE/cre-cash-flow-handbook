## Purpose

The exit cap rate is the rate a future buyer is assumed to pay, applied to
terminal net operating income to produce the sale price. It is the most
consequential assumption in most models, because it sets a number that nothing
inside the model can check.

## Inputs

The assumed market cap rate at the disposition date.

## Output

A rate, stated as a percentage, at the disposition date.

## Method

Set the rate from the going-in rate, adjusted for the property's age, its
remaining lease term at exit, and the expected market at that date. It is
applied to Terminal Net Operating Income (1.3.1) to price the sale at Gross
Sale Price (1.3.2).

## Rules

1. Stated as a percentage and applied at the disposition date.
2. This is an assumption, not a computed result. Nothing in the model derives it.
3. Where the exit rate is set equal to the going-in rate (1.5.11), the entry
   records that as a deliberate assumption rather than leaving it implied.
4. Feeds Development Spread (1.5.16), where it stands for the market cap rate on
   the finished property.
