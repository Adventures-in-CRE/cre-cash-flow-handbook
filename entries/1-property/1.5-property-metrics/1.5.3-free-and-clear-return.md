## Purpose

Free and clear return is the property's operating cash flow for a period divided
by the capital invested to acquire it, with no debt in either term. It is the
unlevered equivalent of cash-on-cash return, and it is the fastest screen there
is for whether a price makes sense.

## Inputs

Cash Flow from Operations (1.2.12). Total Investment Cost (1.1.8).

## Output

A rate, stated as a percentage per year, for a single period.

## Method

Take unlevered cash flow from operations for the period. Divide by total capital
invested. Annualize where the period is shorter than a year.

## Rules

1. `Free and Clear Return = Cash Flow from Operations (1.2.12) / Total
   Investment Cost (1.1.8)`
2. The numerator is after capital expenditure reserves (1.2.10) and after tenant
   improvements and leasing commissions (1.2.11). It is not net operating income.
3. The numerator excludes debt service (1.2.13). The denominator excludes loan
   proceeds and is stated as though the deal were all cash.
4. Stated per period, and annualized where the grid is shorter than a year.
5. Measures one period. It ignores the time value of money and it ignores
   reversion, so it is not a deal-level return.
6. The levered equivalent is Cash-on-Cash Return (1.5.6).
