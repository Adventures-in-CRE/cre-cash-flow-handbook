## Purpose

The levered equity multiple is total cash returned to equity divided by total
equity invested. Leverage shrinks both terms, so the multiple usually falls even
where the levered IRR rises. Reading the two together is what separates a deal
that earns a good rate from one that returns real money.

## Inputs

Net Levered Cash Flow (1.4.2).

## Output

A multiple, stated to two decimal places, for the holding period as a whole.

## Method

Sum every positive cash flow in the levered stream. Sum every negative cash
flow. Divide the first by the absolute value of the second.

## Rules

1. `Equity Multiple = sum of positive cash flows / absolute value of the sum of
   negative cash flows`
2. Computed on Net Levered Cash Flow (1.4.2) over the full holding period,
   including reversion net of loan payoff (1.3.5).
3. Stated as a multiple, not a percentage.
4. Timing does not affect the result.
5. Leverage reduces the denominator and the numerator. A levered multiple below
   the unlevered multiple (1.5.2) is the normal case and is not an error.
6. Excludes entity fees and promote, which are measured in regions 2 and 3.
