## Purpose

Weighted average life is the average time until a loan's principal is repaid,
with each repayment weighted by its size. A lender uses it to compare loans
that amortize on different schedules. It is shorter than the loan term whenever
the loan amortizes, and equal to the term when it does not. It is also called
average life and WAL.

## Inputs

Loan Amortization (1.2.17), specifically the principal repaid in each period.

## Output

A duration, stated in years, for one loan.

## Method

For each period, multiply the principal repaid by the time from origination to
that period. Sum those products. Divide by total principal repaid.

## Rules

1. `WAL = sum(principal repaid in period x years from origination to that
   period) / total principal repaid`
2. Total principal repaid includes the balloon balance repaid at Loan Payoff
   (1.3.5).
3. Time is measured in years from the origination date, using the model's
   period grid.
4. Interest is excluded from both the numerator and the denominator.
5. On an interest only loan, WAL equals the loan term, because all principal is
   repaid at maturity.
6. Scheduled amortization only. Expected prepayment is excluded unless the
   entry states the prepayment assumption used.
