## Purpose

The levered internal rate of return is the annualized rate earned on the equity
actually put into the deal, after the lender is paid. It is higher than the
unlevered rate whenever the property out-earns its cost of debt, and lower
whenever it does not. Gross means before sponsor fees and before promote.

## Inputs

Net Levered Cash Flow (1.4.2), with the date of each cash flow.

## Output

A rate, stated as a percentage per year, for the holding period as a whole.

## Method

Take the levered cash flow stream. Solve for the rate that sets its present
value to zero.

## Rules

1. `IRR = the rate r where the sum over all periods t of (cash flow in t /
   (1 + r)^t) equals zero`
2. Computed on Net Levered Cash Flow (1.4.2).
3. Loan proceeds (1.1.9) reduce the acquisition outflow. Debt service (1.2.13)
   reduces each period. Loan payoff (1.3.5) reduces reversion.
4. Stated as an annual rate, on the same basis as Gross Unlevered IRR (1.5.1).
   The two are only comparable when solved the same way.
5. Excludes sponsor fees (3.15) and promote (3.10.5), measured only in region 3.
6. A stream that changes sign more than once can satisfy the equation at more
   than one rate. The entry reports the root nearest the model's stated initial
   guess and records that the stream is non-conventional.
