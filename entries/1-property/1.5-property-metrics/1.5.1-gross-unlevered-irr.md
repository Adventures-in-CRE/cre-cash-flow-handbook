## Purpose

The internal rate of return is the discount rate at which the net present value
of a cash flow stream is zero. It is the annualized rate earned on capital while
that capital is deployed. Unlevered means before debt. Gross means before sponsor
fees and before promote.

## Inputs

Net Unlevered Cash Flow (1.4.1), with the date of each cash flow.

## Output

A rate, stated as a percentage per year, for the holding period as a whole.

## Method

Take the unlevered cash flow stream, from the acquisition outflow through the
reversion inflow. Solve for the rate that sets its present value to zero.

## Rules

1. `IRR = the rate r where the sum over all periods t of (cash flow in t /
   (1 + r)^t) equals zero`
2. Computed on Net Unlevered Cash Flow (1.4.1), which carries the investment as
   a negative and reversion proceeds as a positive.
3. Stated as an annual rate. Where the grid is not annual, the entry states
   whether it is solved on the grid and annualized or on actual dates.
4. Excludes loan proceeds, debt service, and loan payoff. Those belong to Gross
   Levered IRR (1.5.4).
5. Excludes sponsor fees (3.15) and promote (3.10.5), measured only in region 3.
6. A stream that changes sign more than once can satisfy the equation at more
   than one rate. The entry reports the root nearest the model's stated initial
   guess and records that the stream is non-conventional.
