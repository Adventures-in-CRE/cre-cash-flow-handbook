## Purpose

Loan to cost is the loan amount divided by total project cost. It states how
much of the deal the lender is funding and how much the borrower has at risk. It
is the primary sizing constraint on a development loan, where there is no
value yet to lend against.

## Inputs

Loan Proceeds (1.1.9). Total Investment Cost (1.1.8).

## Output

A rate, stated as a percentage, at a point in time.

## Method

Divide the committed loan amount by total project cost.

## Rules

1. `Loan to Cost = Loan Proceeds (1.1.9) / Total Investment Cost (1.1.8)`
2. The denominator is the full project budget, including land, hard costs, soft
   costs, contingency, and the interest reserve (1.1.12).
3. The numerator is the full loan commitment, including amounts not yet drawn.
4. Where mezzanine debt (1.1.15) is present it is included in the numerator, and
   the ratio is also stated for senior debt alone.
5. On a construction loan the ratio also constrains the Draw Schedule (1.1.13),
   where the loan and equity fund pari passu in each period.
6. Value is not cost. The ratio measured against value is Loan-to-Value (1.5.9).
