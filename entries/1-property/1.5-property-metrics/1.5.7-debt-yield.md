## Purpose

Debt yield is net operating income divided by the loan amount. It is the return
the lender would earn on its money if it foreclosed and took the property.
Because neither term depends on a cap rate or an interest rate, it is the one
loan sizing test that does not move when the market does.

## Inputs

Net Operating Income (1.2.9). Loan Proceeds (1.1.9).

## Output

A rate, stated as a percentage, at a point in time.

## Method

Divide net operating income by the loan amount outstanding at the measurement
date.

## Rules

1. `Debt Yield = Net Operating Income (1.2.9) / loan amount`
2. Net operating income is an annual figure. Where the grid is shorter than a
   year, it is annualized before dividing.
3. The loan amount is the full commitment at origination and the outstanding
   balance from Loan Amortization (1.2.17) thereafter.
4. Where mezzanine debt (1.1.15) is present, the ratio is stated for the senior
   loan alone and for total debt. The two are not interchangeable.
5. Independent of cap rate and interest rate by construction. A variant that
   reintroduces either is not this metric.
