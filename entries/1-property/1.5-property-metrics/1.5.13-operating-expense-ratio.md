## Purpose

The operating expense ratio is operating expenses divided by effective gross
income. It states what share of collected revenue is consumed running the
property. Compared against similar assets, it is the quickest way to see whether
an expense assumption is credible.

## Inputs

Operating Expenses (1.2.8). Effective Gross Income (1.2.7).

## Output

A rate, stated as a percentage, per period.

## Method

Divide operating expenses for the period by effective gross income for the same
period.

## Rules

1. `Operating Expense Ratio = Operating Expenses (1.2.8) / Effective Gross
   Income (1.2.7)`
2. Both terms come from the same period on the same grid.
3. The denominator is effective gross income, after vacancy and the other
   revenue deductions. Potential Gross Income (1.2.1) is not the denominator.
4. The numerator excludes capital expenditure reserves (1.2.10), tenant
   improvements and leasing commissions (1.2.11), and debt service (1.2.13).
5. Comparison between properties requires the same treatment of Expense
   Reimbursements (1.2.15). A property that nets recoveries against expenses
   reports a lower ratio for no economic reason.
