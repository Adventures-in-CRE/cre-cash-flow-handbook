## Purpose

Where the limited partner takes every distribution until it reaches its
preferred return, the sponsor has received nothing while that was being paid.
The catch-up is the tier that lets the sponsor draw a disproportionate share
afterward, until the split between the two reaches the agreed ratio.

## Inputs

Preferred Return (3.10.3) paid to date. The catch-up percentage and the target
promote share.

## Output

One figure per period to the GP, plus a running catch-up balance.

## Method

Once the preferred return is current, direct the catch-up percentage of further
distributions to the GP. Continue until the GP's cumulative share of profit
distributions reaches the target ratio.

## Rules

1. Applies only once Preferred Return (3.10.3) is paid current in the period.
2. `Catch-up amount = distributions in the tier x catch-up percentage`, where
   the percentage is stated in the agreement. A full catch-up is 100 percent to
   the GP.
3. The tier ends when the GP's cumulative profit distributions divided by total
   profit distributions equals the target promote share.
4. Profit distributions exclude Return of Capital (3.10.2). Capital returned is
   not profit and is not caught up on.
5. Where the agreement has no catch-up, this tier is skipped and cash passes
   from 3.10.3 to Promote Tier (3.10.5).
