## Purpose

The preferred return is a return the limited partner earns on its capital
before the sponsor earns any promote. It is a priority, not a guarantee. Where
the deal does not produce the cash, the preferred return accrues and waits.

## Inputs

Distributable Cash (3.10.1). LP Contributions (3.9.2). GP Contributions
(3.9.3). The rate and its compounding terms.

## Output

One figure per period per partner, plus a running unpaid accrual balance.

## Method

Accrue the rate on unreturned capital each period. Pay the accrual from
distributable cash, oldest accrual first. Carry any unpaid amount forward.

## Rules

1. `Accrual in a period = unreturned capital x periodic rate`
2. Unreturned capital is cumulative contributions less cumulative Return of
   Capital (3.10.2), measured at the start of the period.
3. The entry states whether the rate compounds or is simple. The two produce
   different balances and are not interchangeable.
4. The entry states whose capital earns it. Where only LP capital earns it, GP
   Contributions (3.9.3) accrue nothing.
5. Unpaid accrual carries forward and is paid before any later tier. The
   shortfall is tracked by Preferred Return Shortfall (3.14.3).
6. Paid before GP Catch-Up (3.10.4) and before any promote (3.10.5) in the same
   period.
7. This is a distribution priority. Preferred Equity (1.1.16) is a position in
   the capital stack. They are different things.
