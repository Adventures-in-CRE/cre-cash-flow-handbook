## Purpose

The residual split is the last tier of the waterfall. Once every hurdle has been
cleared, the cash that is left is split at the final agreed ratio, with no
further hurdle. It is where the highest promote applies.

## Inputs

Distributable Cash (3.10.1). Distributions in the earlier tiers (3.10.2 to
3.10.5). The final split ratio.

## Output

One LP figure and one GP figure per period, in the model's currency.

## Method

Take the cash left after the earlier tiers and split it at the final ratio.

## Rules

1. `Residual = MAX(Distributable Cash (3.10.1) - Distributions in all earlier tiers (3.10.2 to 3.10.5), 0)`
2. `LP Residual Distribution = Residual x LP Final Share`. `GP Residual
   Distribution = Residual - LP Residual Distribution`.
3. The final shares are those set for the top tier, above the last hurdle. In
   the model the GP share is its capital share plus the top promote on the LP's
   capital share: 10% + 40% x 90% = 46%.
4. No hurdle and no cap. All cash that reaches this tier is split.
5. It is reached only when every earlier tier is paid in full in the period.
6. It is zero in any period where the earlier tiers use all the cash.

## Worked example

The model's waterfall formulas (Investor Returns rows 44 to 96) applied to a
Year 10 Distributable Cash of 12,000,000. The model's own figure is 6,003,534,
which never reaches this tier. Whole dollars.

| Tier | Distributed |
|---|---|
| Preferred return tier | 4,509,683 |
| Second hurdle tier | 3,067,114 |
| Third hurdle tier | 3,528,564 |
| **Residual** | **894,640** |
| LP (54%) | 483,106 |
| GP (46%) | 411,534 |

Tiers add to 12,000,001 because of rounding.
