## Purpose

The promote is the sponsor's share of profit above what its own capital earns.
It is the reason a sponsor does the deal. It pays only once the limited partner
has cleared the hurdles the agreement sets, which is what makes it an incentive
rather than a fee. Also called carried interest and promoted interest.

## Inputs

GP Catch-Up (3.10.4). The hurdle for each tier and the promote share above it.

## Output

One figure per period per partner, split between LP and GP.

## Method

Test whether the LP has reached the tier's hurdle. Where it has, split further
distributions at that tier's promote share until the next hurdle is met, then
move to the next tier.

## Rules

1. Applies only once the tier's hurdle is met, measured on the LP's own cash
   flow stream.
2. `GP promote = distributions in the tier x promote share`. The remainder goes
   to the LP.
3. The hurdle is stated either as an IRR (3.13.8) or as an equity multiple. The
   entry states which. The two do not produce the same tier boundary.
4. Tiers apply in order, lowest hurdle first. Cash fills one tier before any of
   it reaches the next.
5. The promote is not proportional to GP Contributions (3.9.3). It reallocates
   profit rather than returning capital.
6. Promote paid before the LP's final position is known may be recoverable
   under LP Clawback (3.12.1).
