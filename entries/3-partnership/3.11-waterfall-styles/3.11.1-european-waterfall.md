## Purpose

In a European waterfall the sponsor earns no promote until the limited partner
has its capital back and its preferred return paid, measured across the whole
investment. Operating distributions and sale proceeds run through the same
order. Limited partners prefer it because it makes a clawback unlikely.

## Inputs

Distributable Cash (3.10.1) and each distribution tier: Return of Capital
(3.10.2), Preferred Return (3.10.3), GP Catch-Up (3.10.4), Promote Tier
(3.10.5), Residual Split (3.10.6).

## Output

A distribution per partner per period.

## Method

Apply the tiers in order to every distribution, whether it comes from
operations or from a capital event. Cash fills one tier before any of it
reaches the next.

## Rules

1. Tier order is Return of Capital (3.10.2), then Preferred Return (3.10.3),
   then GP Catch-Up (3.10.4), then Promote Tier (3.10.5), then Residual Split
   (3.10.6).
2. Return of capital is measured across the whole investment, not per property
   and not per period.
3. Operating distributions and capital event distributions use the same order.
   There is no separate operating waterfall.
4. No promote is paid until the LP has received its capital back in full and
   its preferred return in full.
5. LP Clawback (3.12.1) is rarely triggered under this style, because promote
   cannot be paid early. It is still stated in the agreement.
