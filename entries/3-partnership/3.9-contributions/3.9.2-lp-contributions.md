## Purpose

The limited partner supplies most of the equity and takes no part in running
the deal. LP contributions are what that partner funds and when. Every
distribution tier that follows is measured against this number, so it is the
base the whole waterfall is built on.

## Inputs

Total Equity Requirement (3.9.1). The LP's committed share. The Capital Call
Schedule (3.9.4).

## Output

One figure per period on the model's period grid, plus a running unreturned
capital balance.

## Method

Apply the LP's share to each period's equity requirement. Carry the cumulative
amount forward as unreturned capital, reduced by return of capital as it is
distributed.

## Rules

1. `LP Contribution in a period = period equity requirement x LP share`
2. Contributions are outflows from the LP and are negative in the LP's own cash
   flow stream.
3. Unreturned capital is cumulative contributions less cumulative Return of
   Capital (3.10.2), measured at each period.
4. The preferred return (3.10.3) accrues on unreturned capital, not on
   committed capital.
5. A commitment is not a contribution. Only funded amounts count, and only from
   the period they are called.
6. Amounts funded out of order or outside the schedule are handled by Irregular
   Contributions (3.14.2).
