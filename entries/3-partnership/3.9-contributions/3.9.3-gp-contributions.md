## Purpose

The general partner funds a small share of the equity alongside the limited
partner. It is called co-invest, and its purpose is alignment: the sponsor has
its own money at risk in the deal it manages. The share is small, and the
promote the sponsor earns later is not proportional to it.

## Inputs

Total Equity Requirement (3.9.1). The GP's committed share. The Capital Call
Schedule (3.9.4).

## Output

One figure per period on the model's period grid, plus a running unreturned
capital balance.

## Method

Apply the GP's share to each period's equity requirement. Carry the cumulative
amount forward as unreturned capital.

## Rules

1. `GP Contribution in a period = period equity requirement x GP share`
2. GP share plus LP share (3.9.2) equals 100 percent of the equity requirement
   in every period.
3. Contributions are outflows from the GP and are negative in the GP's own cash
   flow stream.
4. GP capital receives Return of Capital (3.10.2) on the same terms as LP
   capital unless the agreement subordinates it.
5. Whether GP capital earns the preferred return is stated in Preferred Return
   (3.10.3). It is not assumed either way.
6. The promote (3.10.5) is separate from the return on this capital and is not
   proportional to the GP share.
