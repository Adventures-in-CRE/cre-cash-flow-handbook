## Purpose

Total equity requirement is all the cash the owners put into the deal over its
life: the equity paid at closing plus every later call to cover a shortfall. It
is the base each partner's total contribution is measured against.

## Inputs

Equity Required at Acquisition (1.1.11). Distributable Cash (3.10.1) in each
period after closing.

## Output

One figure for the whole hold, in the model's currency.

## Method

Take the equity paid at closing. Add the amount by which cash falls short in
each later period.

## Rules

1. `Total Equity Requirement = Equity Required at Acquisition (1.1.11) + the sum of Shortfall over all later periods`
2. `Shortfall = -Distributable Cash (3.10.1)` in a period where Distributable
   Cash is negative. Otherwise zero.
3. Gross, not net. A distribution in one period does not reduce a shortfall in
   another.
4. It is an output of the deal's cash flows, not an input to them. Calls fund
   shortfalls and do not change them.
5. Each partner's total is Total Equity Requirement x that partner's share
   (3.9.2, 3.9.3).
6. When the calls happen is set by the Capital Call Schedule (3.9.4), not here.
7. It is larger than Equity Required at Acquisition whenever any later period
   falls short.

## Worked example

Example, in whole dollars. Years 3 to 10 have positive cash, so no shortfall.

| Period | Equity or shortfall |
|---|---|
| Closing (1.1.11) | 2,657,125 |
| Year 1 shortfall | 281,825 |
| Year 2 shortfall | 75,643 |
| **Total Equity Requirement** | **3,014,593** |

At a 90% LP share and 10% GP share, the LP total is 2,713,134 and the GP total
is 301,459.
