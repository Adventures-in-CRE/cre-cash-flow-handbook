## Purpose

A crystallization resets the partners' ownership shares part-way through a deal
so the sponsor can realize its promote without a sale. It suits value-add and
opportunistic investments where most of the value is created early, and it lets
the sponsor carry its earned share forward as ownership rather than wait for an
exit.

## Inputs

Promote Tier (3.10.5). The crystallization date and the value assumed at that
date.

## Output

A revised ownership share per partner, effective from the crystallization date.

## Method

Run a hypothetical sale at the crystallization date value through the
waterfall. Read each partner's share of those hypothetical proceeds. Reset the
ownership percentages to those shares from that date forward.

## Rules

1. `New ownership share = the partner's share of the hypothetical distribution
   at the crystallization date`
2. The hypothetical sale runs the full waterfall, including every promote tier,
   exactly as an actual sale would.
3. No cash moves at crystallization. Only the ownership percentages change.
4. From the crystallization date the promote resets. Further distributions
   follow the new shares until the next crystallization or a sale.
5. The entry states how the crystallization value is set, whether by appraisal,
   by a stated cap rate applied to net operating income, or by an agreed
   formula.
6. Where ownership shares change for other reasons, that is Dynamic Capital
   Stack (3.12.3).
