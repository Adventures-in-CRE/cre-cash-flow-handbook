## Purpose

A clawback returns promote to the limited partner where the deal ended worse
than the interim distributions implied. It exists because an American waterfall
pays the sponsor early, and early payment can prove unearned once the whole
investment is settled.

## Inputs

Promote Tier (3.10.5) distributions paid to date. Preferred Return (3.10.3)
achieved by the LP over the investment.

## Output

One figure at the end of the investment, paid by the GP to the LP.

## Method

Recompute what the GP would have received had the whole waterfall been run once
on the investment's total cash flows. Compare that against what it actually
received. The excess is returned.

## Rules

1. `Clawback = promote actually distributed to the GP - promote the GP would
   have received on total cash flows`, floored at zero.
2. Tested once, at the end of the investment, unless the agreement states
   interim test dates.
3. Returns promote only. Return of Capital (3.10.2) and the return on GP
   Contributions (3.9.3) are not clawed back.
4. The entry states whether the obligation is before-tax or after-tax. A
   clawback capped at the GP's after-tax promote returns less than the gross
   figure.
5. Reduces GP Net Profit (3.13.6) and increases LP Net Profit (3.13.5) in the
   final period.
