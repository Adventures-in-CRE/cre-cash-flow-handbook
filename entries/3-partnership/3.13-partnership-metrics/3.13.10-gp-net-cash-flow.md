## Purpose

GP net cash flow is the general partner's money in and out of the deal, one
figure per period. Contributions are negative. Distributions, including the
promote, and sponsor fees are positive. It is the series the GP's return
measures are computed from.

## Inputs

GP Contributions (3.9.3). The GP's distributions from the waterfall (3.11.1 or
3.11.2). Sponsor fees (3.15.1 to 3.15.4).

## Output

One figure per period, in the model's currency, on the model's period grid.

## Method

In each period, add the GP's distributions and sponsor fees, then subtract the
GP's contributions.

## Rules

1. `GP Net Cash Flow = GP Distributions + Sponsor Fees - GP Contributions`,
   per period
2. GP Distributions is everything the GP receives from the waterfall in the
   period: its share of each tier and the promote (3.10.4, 3.10.5).
3. Sponsor fees are paid to the GP outside the waterfall. Where none are
   charged they are zero.
4. Fees reduce Distributable Cash (3.10.1) before the waterfall, so they are
   not part of GP Distributions. Each fee is counted once.
5. A contribution, distribution or fee in the same period nets into one
   figure.
6. This is the final stream, after every tier. Hurdle tests inside the
   waterfall use the stream through the prior tier, not this one.

## Worked example

Example, in whole dollars. No sponsor fees are charged. Years 4 to 9 are
omitted.

| Year | Contributions | Distributions | Fees | **GP Net Cash Flow** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 265,713 | 0 | 0 | **(265,713)** |
| 1 | 28,183 | 0 | 0 | **(28,183)** |
| 2 | 7,564 | 0 | 0 | **(7,564)** |
| 3 | 0 | 19,140 | 0 | **19,140** |
| 10 | 0 | 869,247 | 0 | **869,247** |

All eleven years sum to 708,702, the GP's total profit.
