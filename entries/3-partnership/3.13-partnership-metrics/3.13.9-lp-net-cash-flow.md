## Purpose

LP net cash flow is the limited partner's money in and out of the deal, one
figure per period. Contributions are negative and distributions are positive.
It is the series the LP's return measures are computed from.

## Inputs

LP Contributions (3.9.2). The LP's distributions from the waterfall (3.11.1 or
3.11.2).

## Output

One figure per period, in the model's currency, on the model's period grid.

## Method

In each period, subtract the LP's contributions from the LP's distributions.

## Rules

1. `LP Net Cash Flow = LP Distributions - LP Contributions`, per period
2. A period with only a capital call is negative. A period with only a
   distribution is positive.
3. LP Distributions is everything the LP receives from the waterfall in the
   period: return of capital, preferred return, and its share of each later
   tier (3.10.2 to 3.10.6).
4. Distributions are after the GP's promote (3.10.5) has been paid.
5. A contribution and a distribution in the same period net into one figure.
6. This is the final stream, after every tier. Hurdle tests inside the
   waterfall use the stream through the prior tier, not this one.
7. Sponsor fees (3.15) are not part of it.

## Worked example

From the A.CRE Apartment Acquisition Model v2.5, Investor Returns, LP row,
rounded to whole dollars. Years 4 to 9 are omitted.

| Year | Contributions | Distributions | **LP Net Cash Flow** |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 2,391,413 | 0 | **(2,391,413)** |
| 1 | 253,643 | 0 | **(253,643)** |
| 2 | 68,079 | 0 | **(68,079)** |
| 3 | 0 | 172,259 | **172,259** |
| 10 | 0 | 5,134,287 | **5,134,287** |

All eleven years sum to 3,689,388, the LP's total profit in the model.
