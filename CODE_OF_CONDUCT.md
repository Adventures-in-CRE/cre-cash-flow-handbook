# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Argue about the methodology. Do not argue about the person.

## What we expect

Disagreement is the point of this project. Commercial real estate practice
varies, people are trained differently, and a contributor who thinks an entry
is wrong is doing the handbook a favor by saying so. Say it about the work.

Specifically:

- Critique rules, methods, and numbers. Not the contributor's competence,
  employer, credentials, or seniority.
- Assume the other person has seen deals you have not.
- When you are shown to be wrong, say so plainly and move on. Nobody here loses
  standing for changing their mind.
- Bring evidence when you can. "That is not how it is done" is not an argument.
  "Here is a worked example where your rule breaks" is.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments, and sustained disruption of
review. Publishing someone's private information. Using the repository to
promote a product, recruit, or attack a competitor.

Deliberately submitting rules you believe to be wrong, or padding contributions
to inflate a record, is a form of vandalism and is treated as one.

## Scope

This applies in pull requests, issues, discussions, and anywhere someone is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the steward listed in
[.github/CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS). Reports are handled privately.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or reject contributions and comments that violate
this document, and may block a contributor from the repository. Repeated or
severe violations lead to a permanent ban. Maintainers who do not follow this
document lose their maintainer status.
